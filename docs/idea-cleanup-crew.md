# Idea: city cleanup crew

Bartosz's idea (2026-11-17): solo or as a crew, you clean up a city after waves of destruction (a monster or something else), sell the debris at your HQ, and buy bigger trucks and better gear.
This file checks it against Roblox games that already exist and against everything in [dead-roblox-games.md](dead-roblox-games.md), then proposes twists. Numbers are from RoWatcher, Rolimons and Rotrends as read on 17 Nov 2026; *(unverified)* marks inference.

## 2. Tested against our research

| Lesson from the research | How this idea does |
|---|---|
| Pure collect-sell-upgrade loops die in 5-10 hours | **The big danger.** Dig & Clean went from 270K to 307 in three months; Clean the WORLD from 10K to 0. The cleaning loop alone is a spike, not a game |
| Survivors have an event calendar | **Strong fit.** Each destruction wave is an event by nature; a new monster or disaster each week is the calendar (Car Crushers 2 lives on exactly this) |
| Co-op loot-sell-upgrade with a shared vehicle works | **Strong fit.** Dead Rails (6.7B visits) is the closest proven shape: crew, shared vehicle, loot, sell, upgrade |
| Waves as a clock pull huge numbers | **Fit.** Escape Tsunami proved a visible incoming wave drives players |
| Must work without chat and on phones | Good: tap to grab, drive with a stick, roles via pings. Risk: driving and physics debris on cheap phones |
| Retention past day 8-28 decides Roblox recommendations | Needs a long goal beyond buying the next truck (see twists) |
| Avoid licensed monsters | Original monsters only (Godzilla-like = takedown risk) |
| Exploiters kill games | Selling and debris value must be decided by the server |

**Verdict: possible and worth it, but only with a hook beyond cleaning.** The cleaning loop is proven to spike (270K) and proven to collapse. The waves, the crew and the trucks are what can make it last, so they are the core, not decoration.

## 3. Twists (staying within the idea)

1. **Clean while the attack is still on.** The monster or disaster is still moving through the city while you work. The richest debris is closest to it. You see it coming (the Hitchcock bomb idea from the TikToks) and decide how greedy to be. This turns a chill sim into tense, clip-worthy rounds, and the wave is the clock (Escape Tsunami proof).
2. **Monster leftovers are the collection.** Every monster drops rare parts (scales, teeth, eggs, glowing cores) mixed into the rubble. They go in a trophy hall at your HQ for friends to see. A new monster each week means new trophies (Dig & Clean's museum hook, plus the weekly event calendar).
3. **The crew truck grows like a base.** One shared crew truck you upgrade together: bigger bed, a crane, a crusher, a magnet. Like Dead Rails' train. Heavy debris needs two players to carry or one crane, so friends matter.
4. **Rebuild the city.** Cleared districts get rebuilt and reopen with new shops, jobs and harder zones. A visible, permanent city that you restored is the long goal past day 8, and gives "unfinished business" to come back for.
5. **Solo players hire a bot crew.** Hired helpers carry and sort debris, weaker than friends. Solves solo play, as we planned for the tandem idea.

Things to avoid: a pure idle cleaning sim, real-time physics destruction of whole buildings (phones can't handle it; use pre-broken pieces), paid upgrades that make friends useless, copying any monster franchise.
