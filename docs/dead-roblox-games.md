# Dead Roblox games research

The "dead game method" from [tiktok-advice.md](tiktok-advice.md), applied to **Roblox games only**: which Roblox games got big, why they grew, why they died, and what players still miss. Goal: find a clear opening for our game.
Researched 2026-11-17. The tandem bike idea is parked (see [game-design.md](game-design.md)).

**How to read the numbers:** visits, peak players (CCU = players online at once) and current players come from Rolimons and RoWatcher pages read on 2026-11-17. Those sites only track CCU from about 2020, so peaks for older games are "tracked-era" peaks, not their real 2010s peaks. Causes marked *(unverified)* are inferred, not sourced.

## 4. Patterns

**How long hits last** ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/one-year-on-the-charts-what-really-happens-after-a-roblox-game-goes-viral)):
- Mega-hits peak and collapse within 3-6 months, losing 96-99%.
- Copycats die within about 7 months.
- About 80% of 2024's top-50 games were outside the top 200 by early 2026.
- Simulators lose about 60% in 30 days without events; horror loses 70-80% in 60 days; roleplay and RPGs are the flattest.

**Why Roblox games die, most common first:**
1. The trend moved on and players migrated (Meep City to Brookhaven, every brainrot game).
2. The loop ran out (simulators exhaust in 5-10 hours).
3. Updates and events stopped (Rivals, Apocalypse Rising, Mad Studio games).
4. The developer replaced it with a sequel or a split version (Pet Sim X, Grow a Garden 2, Apocalypse Rising 2, Booga Booga).
5. Exploiters (Mad Murderer, Darkness 2, Apocalypse Rising).
6. Copyright takedowns of licensed characters (Pokémon games, anime games).
7. Pay-to-win backlash (Pet Sim X, Fisch).
8. Platform rule changes (2018 Filtering Enabled cutoff, 2026 chat age checks).

**What survivors share:**
1. A player trading economy (Adopt Me, Murder Mystery 2, Blox Fruits).
2. A calendar of events; the strongest single predictor of surviving 12 months.
3. Replayable rounds instead of a loop that runs out (Flee the Facility, Evade, Natural Disaster Survival).
4. Regular visible updates (3-4x more players kept at six months).
5. A real community (10K+ group members at peak).

**The "classics re-peaking" signal, checked:** several old games hit new tracked peaks in 2025-2026, but most of it was promotion, not a trend.
- Work at a Pizza Place, Lumber Tycoon 2 and Apocalypse Rising 2 spiked during Roblox's own event "The Hunt: Roblox 20" (Sep 17-28, 2026), which sent players through 20 games in sequence ([Roblox](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/join-the-hunt-roblox-20)). Pizza Place then dropped 57% in a week ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-week-in-numbers-2026-w40)).
- Bee Swarm's spike was its yearly Christmas event ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/bee-swarm-simulator-how-one-developer-built-a-roblox-empire-that-won-t-quit)).
- Only Flee the Facility kept a real chunk (about 22% of its 91K peak, still growing month over month); cause unknown.
- What is real: since June 2026 Roblox recommendations score retention on day 1, days 2-7 and **days 8-28** separately, plus session quality and spending ([Roblox](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)). That rewards any game that keeps players for weeks, classic or not.

**Verdict:** "old formats get a boost" is not a plan. "Keep players past day 8, with a live event calendar" is.

## 5. The openings (loved, now dead or faded, no strong modern replacement)

| # | Game type | Evidence | Why it could work now | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Party minigame collection** (Epic Minigames, Ripull, Plates of Fate) | 2.36B visits, spiked to 32K in 2025, now ~1K; no big modern one found | Short rounds, social, works without chat, easy to add a new minigame every week for events | Needs lots of small minigames; each must feel good |
| 2 | **Social hangout with homes, pets and minigames** (Meep City) | 170K peak to 169 now; Brookhaven covers roleplay only | Social games retain best; homes and pets give things to show off and trade | Needs constant events; Brookhaven is a giant neighbor |
| 3 | **Round-based disaster/hazard survival** (Natural Disaster Survival, Super Bomb Survival) | NDS hit a new 129K peak in 2025 | Cheap to make, replayable rounds, events slot in as new disasters | Easy to copy, low ceiling |
| 4 | **Sky-island building with progression** (Islands) | 106K peak to 658, no successor found | Building plus a goal; long-lasting loop with friends | Big build, economy exploits |
| 5 | **Chapter horror with collectible skins** (Piggy) | 533K peak to 2.3K | Episodic horror lasts 12-24 months longer than one-shot horror | Horror drops fast between chapters |

Honorable mention: classic sword and brick fights (SFOTH, Doomspire): players keep asking for remakes, but the evidence of demand is thin.

**Things to avoid, from the evidence:** licensed characters (takedowns), making our own sequel later (splits the community), a pure simulator loop (burns out in hours), relying on chat (half of players can't), pay-to-win.

## 7. Market check of the top 3 openings (17 Nov 2026)

Live numbers from [RoWatcher genre pages](https://rowatcher.com/genres); approximate, since per-game pages disagree a little.

| Opening | Who's live now | Leader to beat | Content a small team must make | Works without chat / on phones | Money |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Disaster survival rounds** | [Survive Your Ideas](https://rowatcher.com/games/10769671316/survive-your-ideas) (new Oct 2026, 108K peak, ~47K now; a random player types a word and it becomes the hazard), [Natural Disaster Survival](https://rowatcher.com/games/65241/natural-disaster-survival) (2008, 6-13K), [Evade](https://rowatcher.com/games/3647333358/hlwn-evade) 49K, Flee the Facility 13K; old clones dead (Survive The Disasters 2: 43 players) | One fresh hit; the classic is old and under-updated | **Lowest**: a disaster is a script plus effects; one new disaster a week is realistic | Excellent | Medium |
| **Party minigames** | Squid Game X 15-19K, Timebomb Duels 10-17K, Button Battles and Wacky Games (new, 5-12K within weeks), Musical Chairs; Epic Minigames out of the top 15 | Nobody above ~20K; very fragmented | Medium-high: 15-30 minigames at launch, one new a week | Very good | Medium |
| **Social hangout with homes and pets** | Brookhaven ~190-340K, Adopt Me ~220K, Berry Avenue 46K, LifeTogether 32K; Meep City out of the top 15 | Two giants with huge habits | **Highest**: houses, furniture, pets, events | **Weakest**: roleplay depends on chat | Highest |

**Ranking:** 1. disaster survival rounds, 2. party minigames, 3. social hangout.
Survival wins on proven demand (a brand-new hit in Oct 2026), weak old incumbents, the cheapest weekly content and full support for players without chat. Minigames are a close second but the ceiling looks lower (new hits stall at 5-12K). Social hangouts are owned by two giants and need chat.

**Warning:** Survive Your Ideas is a fresh trend. Copycats die within months, so we must not be "another word-to-disaster game". The twist has to be our own.

## 6. Gaps in this research
