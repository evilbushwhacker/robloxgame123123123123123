# Dead game research

The "dead game method" from [tiktok-advice.md](tiktok-advice.md): study multiplayer games that died, find what made them fun, why they died and what players still miss, then reinvent the best of it as an original Roblox game.
Started 2026-11-17. The tandem bike idea is parked (see [game-design.md](game-design.md)); this research decides the next direction.

Reliability: facts with a source link were read from that page. Anything marked *(unverified)* is memory or a weak source.

## 1. Old MMOs and kids' virtual worlds (2000s-2010s)

| Game (closed) | What people loved | Why it died | Proof players still miss it |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Club Penguin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Penguin) (2017) | Minigames earn coins for clothes and igloo decor, pets, **monthly parties with limited rooms and items**, safe chat | Disney cut staff in 2015 over declining popularity, then replaced it with a new app that also died | Fan remake Club Penguin Rewritten hit 11M+ users before police shut it in 2022 |
| [Toontown Online](https://pcgamer.com/10-years-after-it-died-toontown-online-is-more-alive-than-ever) (2013) | **Co-op turn-based combat with joke "gags"**; mixing gags into combos; chat from preset phrases only | Disney's switch to mobile, not the game itself | Toontown Rewritten still running, ~1,700 online at a 2023 event; more fan servers |
| [LEGO Universe](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/lack-of-subscribers-forces-em-lego-universe-em-offline) (2012) | Building inside an adventure world | Too few players paid the subscription | Fan server Darkflame Universe |
| [Glitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glitch_(video_game)) (2012) | No combat; co-op crafting, farming, cooking; world shaped by players | Hard for new players, shallow for veterans | Code released free; 3 fan projects |
| [City of Heroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Heroes) (2012) | Deep costume creator; sidekicking so different levels can team up; player-made missions | Publisher changed focus | Fan server Homecoming became officially licensed in 2024 |
| [Star Wars Galaxies](https://www.pcgamer.com/star-wars-galaxies-devs-discuss-why-it-died) (2011) | Player crafting and economy, about a third of players ran a shop, player-run events | A redesign removed the systems people loved | *(revival servers unverified)* |
| [Free Realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Realms) (2014) | 15 jobs on one character, free decorated home, gentle combat; 10M+ registered | Publisher moved resources ("kids spend little") | None found |
| [Moshi Monsters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshi_Monsters) (2019) | Adopt a monster, daily puzzles for coins, decorate a room, collect mini creatures; 80M+ users | Revenue fell from £46.9M (2012) to £5.2M (2018); Flash ended | Mobile relaunch Kickstarter, 2027 |
| [FusionFall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FusionFall) (2013) | Collectible mini characters that grant abilities | No reason given | Fan remakes killed by takedowns |
| [Pirates of the Caribbean Online](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean_Online) (2013) | Ship battles, cards, fishing, potion minigames | Disney's mobile switch | Fan revival in open beta |

**Pattern:** these mostly died for **business reasons** (owner changed focus, subscriptions, Flash), not because players stopped liking them. Fan revivals keep popping up and getting shut down by the owners. That's demand with no legal home.

| Game | Signature mechanic | Why it died | Players still miss it |

| [Paragon](https://pcgamer.com/paragon-is-closing-in-april-epic-offers-full-refunds-to-everyone) (2018) | MOBA | Team moved to Fortnite | Petition, fan revival *(unverified)* |
| [Splitgate](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/fps/with-steam-players-down-over-90-percent-splitgate-2-dev-un-releases-the-game-lays-off-more-staff-and-plans-to-shut-down-og-servers-as-fans-fear-another-live-service-death-we-understand-its-our-last-chance) | Portals plus shooting | Sequel lost 90%+ of players | - |
| [Fall Guys](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/fall-guys-hit-50-million-players-two-weeks-after-freemium-switch) (counterexample) | Party obstacle royale | Nearly died, came back: going free brought 50M players in 2 weeks | - |

**Death causes, most common first:** owner changed priorities; launched into a crowded genre with weak marketing; too few players to fill big lobbies; content droughts; too hard to learn; wrong price.

## 3. Old Roblox hits that faded

| Game | What was great | Why it faded |
|---|---|---|
| [Apocalypse Rising](https://brainmindsociety.com/blog/the-downfall-of-apocalypse-rising) | Hardcore survival, lose your loot on death, tense scavenging | Developer moved on, rare updates, exploiters, newer-looking rivals |
| [Super Bomb Survival](https://rowatcher.com/games/78351145/super-bomb-survival/dying) | Short chaotic bomb rounds | No content refresh *(unverified)*; ~90 players now |
| Vehicle Simulator, Sandbox 1/2, Starscape, Undersiders, Obby Master ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/dead-experiences-that-had-potential/2064795)) | Driving; free building; space exploration; tunnel-and-mine capture the flag; 60-second obby maps | Update silence, developer problems, a big map update that drove players off, sequel split the community |
| [Steal a Brainrot](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals) | Stealing from other players' bases | Down 99.2% from its 25.8M peak; trend burnout |
| Adopt Me, Jailbreak (still alive) | Trading economy; cops and robbers | Slow decline from huge peaks (700K and 100K+) |

**What decides survival on Roblox** ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/one-year-on-the-charts-what-really-happens-after-a-roblox-game-goes-viral)):
- About 80% of 2024's top-50 games were outside the top 200 by early 2026.
- Games updated recently keep 3-4x more players at six months.
- Seasonal events are the strongest single predictor of surviving 12 months.
- Simulators and tycoons run dry in 5-10 hours; social and fashion games retain best; episodic horror lives 12-24 months longer than one-shot horror.
- Top studios bank 2-3 months of updates before chasing visibility.

## 4. The openings, ranked

Scored on: players proven to miss it, nobody on Roblox clearly serves it, works on phones with short sessions, a small team can keep it updated, it fits Roblox's 2026 rules (chat limits, 16+ launch).

| # | Opening | Evidence | Fit with 2026 Roblox | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Co-op "gag" combat with combos** (Toontown) | Fan servers alive 13 years after shutdown | Preset-phrase chat was Toontown's design; Roblox's age-checked chat makes that a feature. Taps, not aiming, so great on phones | Needs a lot of content (enemies, zones); turn-based is unusual on Roblox |
| 2 | **Social world with a monthly event calendar and minigames** (Club Penguin) | 11M+ on a fan remake; owners keep shutting revivals | Social games retain best, events predict survival, social-idle is the rising genre | Needs a steady stream of events; Brookhaven and Adopt Me partly cover it |
| 3 | **Team dodgeball with catch, curve and pass** (Knockout City) | 12M players, official private servers | Small 3v3 lobbies fill fast; Blade Ball proves the audience likes deflect-and-dodge | Competitive games attract exploiters; need to check Roblox dodgeball competition |
| 4 | **Player-run shops and crafting** (Star Wars Galaxies, Glitch) | A third of players ran shops | Trading is huge on Roblox | Economy exploits and duping; Roblox already has trading-heavy games |
| 5 | **Hazard-round survival with seasons** (Natural Disaster Survival, Super Bomb Survival) | NDS is still alive after 10+ years | Very cheap to make, short rounds | Easy to copy, low ceiling |

**Lesson from the tandem idea:** before choosing, every opening has to be checked against what is already live on Roblox. We missed that once and found three tandem games after designing ours.
